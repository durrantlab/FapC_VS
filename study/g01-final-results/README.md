
# g01-final-results
Goal: based on visual inspection of diversity and experimental set, compile a list of ~100 molecules into a csv / SDF to send to collaborators
- Input: annotated CSVs about top molecules, all top molecules in their own SDFs
- Output: CSV with top molecules listed, concat SDF with all of them (same order)

## Data
part1-diversity/ holds information about top compounds for the diversity set pipeline
- concat_molecules: top 100 in 1 SDF together
- fapc_ranked_top_100: statistics about top 100 and whether they were included or not
- seperated_molecules: top 100 in seperate SDFs
